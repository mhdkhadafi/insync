Join us for a **Freewriting Evening at The Queensboro!**

Most of us have things we want to write. We just never quite find the time.

Write With Your Community is a dedicated evening to finally put words on the page. INSYNC hosts this relaxed freewriting session at The Queensboro, where simple prompts give you a clear place to start and a warm room to write in.

Bring a notebook. Get a prompt. Write. It's that simple.

Come as you are. Settle in with a light dinner or a drink, the kitchen will be open.
